# Product card

The single source of truth for every submission. Fill this in once per
product, then paste from it into every form — never draft new marketing
copy inside a directory's submission form. A form asking for content this
card doesn't cover (a long "Key Features" essay, "Use Cases", "Security")
is a reason to skip that site, not a reason to invent text on the spot.

## Template

- **Name:**
- **Website URL:**
- **One-liner** (≤60 chars — some directories hard-cap this field):
- **Short description** (~50 words):
- **Long description** (~150 words):
- **Features list** (3-6 concrete, verifiable items — no "best", "fastest",
  or other comparative/quality claims):
- **Category** (in priority order — use whichever the site's taxonomy has):
- **Tags:**
- **Pricing model** (Free / Freemium / Paid / Free trial — pick the one the
  site's own definition actually matches; don't round up):
- **Logo:** square PNG or JPG, one canonical file, reused everywhere. Name
  it something stable like `logo-square.png` and never substitute the
  website's favicon or an old/unapproved logo for it.
- **Founder / company:** company name only. Do not list a personal
  founder name or handle unless the site requires it and the person has
  agreed to appearing publicly.
- **Support email:** a public product/support inbox, not a personal one.

## Example — ReelsCut

Canonical copy, verbatim, as actually submitted to launch directories.

- **Name:** ReelsCut
- **Website URL:** `https://reelscut.ai/` (app: `https://app.reelscut.ai/`)
- **One-liner:**

  > Turn raw vertical talking-head footage into a finished short.

- **Short description** (49 words):

  > ReelsCut edits raw vertical talking-head footage. Upload the file you
  > recorded, with retakes, false starts and pauses left in. ReelsCut selects
  > among repeated takes, trims false starts and adds captions, then returns
  > one finished 9:16 short. You watch a full watermarked preview before you
  > pay for the clean file.

- **Long description** (148 words):

  > ReelsCut edits raw vertical talking-head footage: one person speaking to
  > the camera, filmed on a phone in 9:16.
  >
  > You upload the recording as you filmed it, with repeated takes, abandoned
  > starts and pauses still in. Nothing needs to be pre-cut. ReelsCut selects
  > among the repeated lines, trims false starts and adds captions, then
  > assembles a single finished short. What comes back is a publishable
  > video, not a project file for another editor.
  >
  > The first render is a full watermarked preview. You watch it end to end
  > and decide before anything is charged. Payment unlocks the clean,
  > watermark-free file.
  >
  > Revisions are written in plain English, such as "shorter intro" or "keep
  > the first take". There are up to two rounds before payment and up to ten
  > after the clean file is unlocked. Each round re-renders the video.
  >
  > ReelsCut covers plain talking-head takes: not screen recordings,
  > green-screen or picture-in-picture footage.

- **Features list:**
  - Selects among repeated takes automatically
  - Trims false starts and long pauses
  - Adds captions
  - Full watermarked preview before payment
  - Plain-English revision requests, re-rendered per round

- **Category** (priority order): Video Editing → AI Video → Short-form
  Video → Social Media Tools → Content Creation.

- **Tags:** talking head, vertical video, 9:16, short-form video, captions,
  retakes, filler words, reels, shorts, tiktok.

- **Pricing model:** Paid. When a form forces a single label, use `Paid`
  and add one line in the description field: "Full watermarked preview
  before payment; paid to unlock the clean file." Don't select `Freemium`
  or `Free trial` — the free part is a preview, not a tier or a trial.

- **Founder / company:** Applied Miracles LLC.
- **Support email:** support@reelscut.ai

What's intentionally left out: speed claims, accuracy claims, comparisons
with competitors, and promises that every cut is perfect. That's a
copywriting boundary, not an omission — it holds in every form this card
gets pasted into.
