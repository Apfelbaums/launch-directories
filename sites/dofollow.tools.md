# dofollow.tools

- **Submit URL:** https://dofollow.tools/submit — a two-step form; step 1 (Tool Information) needs no account, an account is only required at step 2 when picking a plan
- **Login:** Google, GitHub, or email-code — required even for the nominally free plan
- **Captcha / bot protection:** unknown
- **Price:** three paid tiers plus a "Free Listing." The free listing also requires a dofollow badge to stay live on your own site — remove the badge and the link drops to nofollow.
- **Link type:** dofollow only while the reciprocal badge stays live
- **Form fields:** URL, Name, tagline (61 chars), description (canonical ~150-word text), Logo, Categories (Video / Content Creators / Social Media), Pricing Model → Paid, Platforms → Web, founder's social link (skipped)
- **Logo & limits:** upload worked and was verified by screenshot preview
- **Email confirmation:** unknown
- **Moderation:** unknown — never reached
- **Pitfalls:** a Google account chooser can default to the wrong signed-in Google account on a shared browser profile — don't sign into a third-party directory under the wrong one. Choosing "use another account" can trigger a passkey prompt with no password fallback, which an automated session can't complete. Separately, the free-tier daily submission quota can be exhausted independent of login state (visible even before signing in) and resets daily.
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-28 — Needs a human: sign-in requires a passkey (no password on file) and the free-tier daily submission quota was full; step-1 form data was fully prepared and validated, ready to submit once a human completes login. Not pursued further (paid options not used).
