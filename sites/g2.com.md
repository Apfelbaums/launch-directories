# g2.com

- **Submit URL:** https://www.g2.com/products/new — a single shared form that also publishes to Capterra, GetApp, and Software Advice at once; there's no separate Capterra submission path anymore
- **Login:** Google OAuth works on a clean browser profile, but G2 additionally requires business verification — a personal Gmail login can reach the account dashboard but not open the actual listing form ("You must be validated through LinkedIn or business email to proceed"). Worked around by creating an account with a company-domain email instead (the confirmation link arrived within seconds).
- **Captcha / bot protection:** none encountered
- **Price:** unknown — never reached pricing
- **Link type:** unknown
- **Form fields:** reached "Product or pricing page URL" before being blocked
- **Logo & limits:** not reached
- **Email confirmation:** yes, for the business-email signup path — a one-time confirmation link
- **Moderation:** an automatic eligibility scan runs right after the product URL is entered
- **Pitfalls:** the scan flatly rejected the product as B2C: "Looks like this is a business-to-consumer (B2C) product. G2 only accepts business-to-business (B2B) products." This is a platform business rule, not a technical or login obstacle, and there's no workaround — a product serving individual content creators rather than companies is genuinely B2C, and no false B2B claim should be made to get past it. Because the same form cross-publishes to Capterra via a checkbox on an earlier step, this single rejection blocks both platforms at once.
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-27 — Not a fit: G2 (and by extension Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice via the same form) only accepts B2B products; revisit only if a genuine B2B offering is added later
