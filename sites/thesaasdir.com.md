# thesaasdir.com

- **Submit URL:** https://thesaasdir.com/submit/ — a four-step form: URL/autofill → Product Details (Name/Tagline/Description/Screenshot/Categories, up to 3/Tags/Email) → Choose Your Plan → Add Badge to Your Site.
- **Login:** none needed, no account, no captcha.
- **Captcha / bot protection:** none.
- **Price:** Free — 1 dofollow link, badge required. Paid $19 — 3 dofollow links + featured, no badge needed. Multiple Listings $29 — also lists on TheAIToolsDir.
- **Link type:** dofollow (Free tier, gated on the badge; guaranteed on paid tiers).
- **Form fields:** Website URL, Tool Name, Tagline, ~148-word description, Categories (from a fixed list — pick 2-3 closest matches, e.g. AI / Design / Other if no video-specific category exists), Tags, Email, Logo upload, plan selection.
- **Logo & limits:** upload works via a data-URI-to-Blob-to-File-to-DataTransfer workaround; a plain upload tool hit a Chrome bug on this site's upload widget once.
- **Email confirmation:** not required.
- **Moderation:** no account/draft mechanism at all — the form doesn't persist state if you navigate away, so it has to be completed in one sitting. On the Free plan, the flow ends with "Verify Badge" (checks that the badge is live on your own site) then "Submit Product", both in the same session.
- **Pitfalls:** the free tier's dofollow link is conditioned on installing the directory's badge on your own site first — that's a site/product decision, not something to do unilaterally. Auto-fill on the categories step may pre-check a wrong category (e.g. Productivity) — review and correct before submitting. Once the badge is actually live, "Verify Badge" confirms it by finding the dofollow link on your site, and "Submit Product" goes live immediately after ("Your badge has been verified and your listing is now live").
