# neeed.directory

- **Submit URL:** https://neeed.directory/submit
- **Login:** Google OAuth
- **Captcha / bot protection:** unknown
- **Price:** free tier ("$0 with our badge") requires installing a Neeed badge on your own site to unlock a dofollow link; Premium $19 paid (skips the badge requirement).
- **Link type:** dofollow (once badge is installed and verified)
- **Form fields:** the submit form can auto-fill from a URL — paste the product URL and click "Prefill" to pull name, tagline, description, site, type, category, logo and cover image from the site's own meta tags. Only Pricing needed a manual fix (defaulted to "Paid", changed to "Freemium").
- **Logo & limits:** pulled automatically via Prefill from site meta tags.
- **Email confirmation:** none noted.
- **Moderation:** after choosing the "$0 with our badge" plan and confirming "I'll add the badge – Submit", the listing goes live immediately once the badge is installed on the product site and "Verify Badge" is clicked on the listing page.
- **Pitfalls:** the free plan is gated on a badge — decide whether to add it to your product's footer/site before submitting through this path, or pay $19 to skip it.
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-27 — Listed; badge installed on the product site, verified, live listing with a dofollow link.
