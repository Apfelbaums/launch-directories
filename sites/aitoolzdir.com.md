# aitoolzdir.com

- **Submit URL:** https://aitoolzdir.com/submit
- **Login:** none required
- **Captcha / bot protection:** unknown
- **Price:** Paid Listing $25 (no backlink required, live in 24h) or Free Listing $0 — the free tier requires a plain-text backlink to aitoolzdir.com already live on your own site BEFORE submitting ("Add this to your site before submitting")
- **Link type:** the required reciprocal link is a plain text link (not an image)
- **Form fields:** Tool Name, Tool URL, Logo URL (a public image URL, not a file upload), Category, Short Description, Full Description, Email
- **Logo & limits:** logo is supplied as a URL rather than uploaded — an existing public og-image URL works fine
- **Email confirmation:** unknown
- **Moderation:** "we'll review your tool and email you when it's live" — no stated timeframe
- **Pitfalls:** the free tier is gated on a reciprocal backlink already being live on your site before you submit — a site-content decision, not a form problem. Once the backlink is live, the rest of the form takes about two minutes.
