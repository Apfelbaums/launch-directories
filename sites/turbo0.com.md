# turbo0.com

- **Submit URL:** https://turbo0.com/submit
- **Login:** email/password account
- **Captcha / bot protection:** none
- **Price:** free tier available, but a dofollow link requires a badge on your own site (see Pitfalls); paid Basic $29.9 and Pro $99.9 publish immediately without a badge.
- **Link type:** dofollow (Free, gated on badge verification; guaranteed on paid tiers)
- **Form fields:** standard product fields plus an "Introduction" field with a hidden minimum length requirement.
- **Logo & limits:** uploads via a fetch→File→DataTransfer workaround; use the actual product logo, not the site's favicon.
- **Email confirmation:** unknown.
- **Moderation:** after filling the form, submission can silently stall on step 1/3 with no visible error toast — the actual cause was a hidden validation rule requiring the "Introduction" field to be at least 1200 characters. Padding it out with real product facts (not invented copy) to clear that threshold let it proceed. After that, the dashboard shows status "Submitting" / "Not published" until you explicitly click "Publish".
- **Pitfalls:** clicking "Publish" opens a plan choice: Free ($0) requires "Verify backlink first" — installing a "Listed on Turbo0.com" badge on your own site and confirming the URL, which is a product/site decision, not something to do unilaterally. Once the badge is live and verified, choosing Free flips status to "Pending" (goes to manual review by the site).
