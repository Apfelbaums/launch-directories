# findly.tools

- **Submit URL:** https://findly.tools/submit
- **Login:** email/password
- **Captcha / bot protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile, passed automatically without intervention
- **Price:** the $0 plan requires placing a badge/backlink on your own site to earn one honored dofollow backlink; paid tiers not evaluated
- **Link type:** dofollow, conditional on the badge being live
- **Form fields:** Category → Video; Pricing Model → Freemium; Audience → "solo content creators"; Access → "Account required"; Platform → Web
- **Logo & limits:** the logo must be a dedicated product logo file, not the site's favicon/touch-icon asset — don't reuse an outdated icon for directory cards
- **Email confirmation:** yes — confirmation email found and completed
- **Moderation:** none beyond badge verification — the listing published immediately after "Verify badge now" succeeded
- **Pitfalls:** the free tier is fully gated behind a live reciprocal badge on your own site; the form itself can be completed and verified by screenshot well before the badge decision is made, so most of the delay is waiting on a site change, not the form.
- **Last checked:** 2026-09-27 — Listed (live, published listing page, confirmed after the reciprocal badge went live)
