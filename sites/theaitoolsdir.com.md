# theaitoolsdir.com

- **Submit URL:** https://theaitoolsdir.com/submit
- **Login:** none, no account needed
- **Captcha / bot protection:** none
- **Price:** Free (1 dofollow link, requires a badge on your own site) / Paid $19 (3 links + featured, no badge needed) / Multiple Listings $29 (adds a listing on TheSaaSDir too).
- **Link type:** dofollow (Free tier, once badge is installed and verified)
- **Form fields:** Product Name, Tagline, Description (49-word style), Category (pick up to 3 from a fixed emoji-labeled list — e.g. Video), Tags (free text, e.g. talking head, vertical video, 9:16, captions, shorts), Email.
- **Logo & limits:** not separately itemized in the source note.
- **Email confirmation:** unknown.
- **Moderation:** confirmation screen reads "Submission Received... We'll verify your status and review your listing within 7 days."
- **Pitfalls:** the badge code is already generated for your product's slug and shown on the form before you even submit — grab it there. The "Verify Badge" button on the site only checks the URL currently typed into a fresh `/submit` form, not an already-submitted listing — there's no separate verify-for-existing-listing flow. Re-submitting to trigger a verify would create a duplicate of an already-pending listing, so don't do that; just wait out the review window, then verify once the badge is actually live on your site.
