- **Captcha / bot protection:** none

- **Moderation:** "we'll review it ASAP", no stated timeframe; inclusion in the gallery is a manual editorial call, not guaranteed.
- **Pitfalls:** none — simplest form in this batch, submit gave an immediate "Thank you! Your submission has been received!" confirmation.
