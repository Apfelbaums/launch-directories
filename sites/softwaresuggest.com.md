- **Login:** the intake form itself requires a "Business Email" (a tooltip specifically flags this as different from a personal email) plus a phone number with country code, Organization and Website — this creates a vendor account.

- **Link type:** unknown
- **Form fields:** Business Email, phone (with country code), Organization, Website — not filled in (see Pitfalls).
